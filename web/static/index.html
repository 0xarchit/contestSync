Frequently asked questions

Is ContestSync free?

Yes — completely free, forever. No premium tier, no ads, no data selling. ContestSync is open source and community-driven.

Which platforms are supported?

LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef, AtCoder, HackerRank, GeeksforGeeks, and Code360. More platforms are added based on community requests.

How does it handle timezones?

Contest times are fetched in UTC and Google Calendar automatically converts them to your local timezone. No manual conversion needed.

What Google permissions does it need?

ContestSync only requests access to your Google Calendar to create contest events. It never reads your existing events, emails, or any other data.

How often does it sync?

Contests are synced daily. New contests from all platforms are automatically fetched and added to your calendar without any action needed from you.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. From the Preferences page, click "Remove Account & Data" to permanently delete your account and all associated data with a single click.

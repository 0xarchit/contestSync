Privacy Policy

At ContestSync, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, protect, and handle your data when you use our sync tool.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum required to perform the synchronization service:

  • Your Google Account email address (to identify you and manage your session).
  • Google Calendar OAuth refresh tokens (encrypted using AES-256) to write and update contest events on your calendar.
  • Your platform sync preferences (e.g., LeetCode, Codeforces).

2. How We Protect Your Data

All sensitive credentials (including OAuth refresh tokens) are encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 with key versioning. We do not store your Google password, and we only request scopes strictly necessary for calendar management.

3. Data Deletion & Autonomy

We believe you should have total control over your data. A permanent account deletion feature is built directly into our platform:

  • You can delete your account at any time via the button in your preferences dashboard.
  • Upon request, our system immediately revokes your Google OAuth refresh token, permanently deletes all user database records, and initiates a clean-up task to delete all synced ContestSync calendar events from your Google Calendar.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. All schedule lookups are done directly against the platforms or our clean public cache.
